How Routing Works

The router selects exactly one route for every question.

DIRECT

For general questions that can be answered using the LLM's existing knowledge.

WIKIPEDIA

For factual and historical or general knowledge queries.

TAVILY

For current or web-based information.

ARXIV

For academic and research-paper queries.
